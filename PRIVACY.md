# Privacy policy

*TunerToGo for Wear OS · last updated 26 October 2026*

TunerToGo does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## Microphone

TunerToGo needs the microphone permission to hear your instrument. The microphone is only
active while the tuner screen is open and the app is in the foreground; it stops as soon as
you leave the tuner, open the menu or the watch goes to sleep.

The sound is analysed in real time on your watch to measure its pitch. Each fragment of
audio (about a tenth of a second) is discarded right after the analysis. Nothing is
recorded, written to storage or transmitted.

## Network

TunerToGo has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere, and it contains no
analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries.

## Settings

Your settings (instrument, tuning, reference pitch and display options) are stored on the
watch only. Like all app settings, they may be included in your Android device backup if
you have backups turned on; this is handled by the operating system, not by TunerToGo.

## Vibration

The vibration permission is used for the short tap when a note is in tune. It can be turned
off in the app's settings.
